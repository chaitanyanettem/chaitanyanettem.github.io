Hi! My name is [Chaitanya]{tooltip="Pronounced: Chai-tuh-nya"}. Welcome to my website! Find my blog [here](/blog/).

I'm a Software Engineer at [Rubrik](https://rubrik.com) where I build complex distributed systems for data protection and storage using C++, Go, and Python. For the last 3 years, I've been one of the tech leads for Atlas, Rubrik's distributed filesystem. Atlas is deployed on thousands of clusters worldwide, both on-premises and in the cloud, with total data under management at exabyte scale.

Previously, I was a founding engineer and tech lead at [Mason](https://getmason.io) (formerly Kubric) from 2017 to 2021, where I built products for scalable content creation. I led development of our ML/AI features such as salient object detection, automatic cropping, image resizing, and background removal. I also built our image/video rendering pipelines and our API platform. Read more about our ML features in this [archived blog post](https://web.archive.org/web/20200930151948/https://reads.kubric.io/blog/eliminate-the-grunt-work-automation-tech-at-kubric/) from 2020.

Earlier in my career (2015 - 2017), I worked as a freelance Android Engineer, building mobile apps for various early-stage companies.
